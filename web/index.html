cognator

Framework-native cognate detection with calibrated confidence

cognator clusters phonologically related forms across lects within a concept, emitting per-prediction confidence backed by a held-out calibration. The full pipeline — merkmal-based phonetic prior, asymmetric alignment, support-aware graph construction, Infomap clustering, and form-local risk bands — runs in your browser as a single WebAssembly module. No installation, no server-side processing.
